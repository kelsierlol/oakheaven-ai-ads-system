# Sales Calls — EMPTY (substitute in effect)

**Oakhaven Media has no sales calls.** No recordings, no transcripts, no CRM history.

The Creative Strategy skill hard-gates on **5 minimum, 10+ ideal** transcripts. That gate is currently being
satisfied by a declared substitute:

→ **`knowledge-base/research/voice-of-customer.md`** — verbatim prospect language harvested from competitor
reviews, forum threads, and ad comments, plus a harvest queue for more.

## This substitute is weaker than the real thing

It gives you market pain language. It does **not** give you:
- Price objections at Oakhaven's actual price point
- What people say no to on a call, and at which moment
- What actually closes
- What existing clients value after buying

Every persona and angle built from the substitute is a **lower-confidence hypothesis**. Say so in the outputs.

## What to do about it

1. **Record and transcribe every sales call from call #1.** No exceptions.
2. Drop transcripts here as `.txt` or `.md`, named `YYYY-MM-DD-<first-name>-<outcome>.md`
   (outcome = `closed` / `no` / `noshow` / `followup`).
3. At **5 transcripts**, re-run Creative Strategy. The personas will change — expect that, it's the point.
4. Aim for diversity, not just volume: different segments (coach / business owner / creator), different
   outcomes (closed and lost), different objection patterns. Ten calls with the same profile is one call.

## Interim proxy, if calls are weeks away

Run 5–8 customer interviews with people in the ICP who aren't clients yet — 20 minutes each. Ask:
what have you tried, what went wrong, what did it cost you, what would make you say no to a service like
this, what would you pay. Transcribe them into this folder marked `interview-` instead of a date. It is not
as good as a real call with money on the table, but it is far better than competitor reviews alone.
