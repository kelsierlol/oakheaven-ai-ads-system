## What this tells us

**Coaches buy — five of them here.** The segment is real.

**Every single one is sold on cost saved and time reclaimed.** Not one mentions premium positioning, charging
more, or looking like the top coach in their niche. The client's three TOF premium angles still have zero
support, and this was the most likely place in the market to find it.

**The proof format is the takeaway.** Named coach + @handle + dollars saved + video count + hours reclaimed.
That's the most concrete social proof in this market and it's the shape Oakhaven's case studies should take.
