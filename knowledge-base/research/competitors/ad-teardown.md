## 🚨 Finding 1 — Oakhaven's VSL v1 is already a competitor's running ad

Not "similar positioning." The same script, beat for beat. Two competitors are running it right now.

**EditCrew — "Ditch the $5k-8k/month Video Editing Bill"** (0:00–0:49)

| Timestamp | What they say | Oakhaven's VSL v1 equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 0:10–0:15 | *"without having to hire an in-house editor"* | The $5–10k payroll anchor |
| 0:15–0:25 | *"an entire creative team dedicated towards you. A producer, project manager, editor"* | "You don't just get an editor… you get a project manager" — but they field **three** |
| 0:20–0:25 | *"delivered within two days"* | 24-hour turnaround |
| 0:25–0:37 | *"unlimited video revisions… all for one fixed price. No hidden fees, no surprises"* | "Flat price, NOT hourly" |
| 0:37–0:46 | *"14-day satisfaction guarantee… If you're not thrilled, we'll refund your money"* | The 14-day 100% satisfaction guarantee |
| 0:46–0:49 | *"book a call today"* | Same CTA |

Even the ad's **title** is Oakhaven's price anchor: *"Ditch the $5k-8k/month video editing bill."*

**Boomin Brands — "20 Videos. 14 Days. Or you don't pay!"** (0:00–0:54)

| Timestamp | What they say |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | *"Tired of burning cash on in-house editors or playing freelancer roulette?"* — **both** Oakhaven pain angles, in the hook |
| 0:13–0:19 | *"an entire creative team… a success manager, a project manager, a dedicated editor"* — three people again |
| 0:19–0:24 | *"you get your videos in less than 24 hours"* |

| 0:39–0:50 | *"14-day money-back satisfaction guarantee… no questions asked"* |
| 0:50–0:54 | *"Book a call"* |

**What this means:** running VSL v1 doesn't put Oakhaven in a crowded market — it puts Oakhaven in the position of paying to say what EditCrew and Boomin already say, with a smaller team and no proof. The claim that Oakhaven fields two people is a *downgrade* against two competitors advertising three.

**VSL v1 is dead. Do not run it, do not adapt it.** VSL v2 (mechanism-led) is the only viable direction, and this is now evidence, not opinion.

## Finding 2 — EditCrew's scaled winner

**`video-bottlenecks-killing-your-growth` is running as 5 simultaneous active variants** (ad IDs 1114911627184537, 1306636241379601, 1587823520018575, 1597323008487219, 2070911607039123).

Five live variants of one hook is what a proven concept looks like — they've found something and are scaling it. The angle is **"video bottlenecks killing your growth"**: growth-framed, not time-framed. Worth studying closely before writing Oakhaven's hooks; it's the closest thing to a validated hook in this market.

Their other live angles: *"Need video editors?"*, *"Time to upgrade your video editing"*, *"On-demand video editing support"*.

## Finding 3 — Boomin Brands' offer beats Oakhaven's

*"20 Videos. 14 Days. Or you don't pay!"* plus *"⚠️ The down side? Very limited spots available."*

Specific number, specific timeframe, risk reversal in the headline, and scarcity. Oakhaven's current offer — "book a 15-min call and get a free video edit" — is softer on every axis. The free edit is a decent lead magnet but the **core offer has no quantified promise**. Worth fixing when the price and volume land from the questionnaire.

## Finding 4 — The Editors Connection sells authority, not benefits

*"Trusted by Hulu, Lancôme, Air China"* — 4 active variants. They're the only one in the set not competing on time/reliability; they compete on **who else hired them**. That's a proof play, and it's available to them because they have the logos.

Oakhaven cannot run this angle — zero named clients. It does show a third lane exists (authority) beyond time-saved and mechanism.

**Data-quality flag:** two ads filed under this competitor (`shop-from-over-30000-products…`) look like unrelated advertisers picked up by the keyword search. Discard them from analysis.

## What this changes

1. **VSL v1 is retired.** Evidence-backed, not a judgment call.
2. **Every table-stakes claim is now confirmed spending, on video, with timestamps.** Dedicated PM, unlimited revisions, fixed price, 14-day guarantee, 24-hour/2-day turnaround, in-house-cost anchor, book-a-call CTA. Using any of these as a *hook* means being outspent saying the same sentence.
3. **The mechanism lane is confirmed empty.** Across 18 ads, not one claims their editors understand what the client *means*. Nobody names a process with phases. The Content Immersion System remains the only open position.
4. **Oakhaven's offer needs a quantified promise** to sit alongside "20 videos in 14 days or you don't pay."
5. **Study the EditCrew bottleneck hook** — 5 live variants, growth-framed, the one validated concept in the set.
