# Proof — EMPTY (largest gap in the workspace)

Guided-setup Phase 5.3 requires at least one proof file here. There are none.
Step 04 sets the bar at **minimum 5 testimonials, case studies, or results with specific numbers.**

## What exists (claims, not proof)

- Founder runs 3 online brands; the same team edits content and ads for them, with the founder's own money behind the spend
- 1,000+ hours of founder training invested in the team
- "Thousands of reels, YouTube videos and ads" delivered

All three are assertions with nothing verifiable attached. No named clients, no before/afters, no retention
numbers, no revision-rate stats, no screenshots.

## What to produce, ranked

1. **The side-by-side.** Same footage, same segment: a generic edit vs. the Oakhaven edit. The entire offer is
   a visual claim being made in a visual medium — right now it has no visual evidence. The client's own VSL
   script marks this as *"your most powerful proof element — use it"* and leaves it blank. Produce this first.
2. **First-draft approval rate.** The VSL leaves a placeholder for it (*"even '87% of our edits are approved on
   the first draft' would be powerful"*). If the number is real, it directly proves the Quality Gate phase.
3. **Brand Download walkthrough** — screen recording of the actual onboarding/brief process. Makes the
   mechanism concrete instead of asserted.
4. **Client results** — any creator or business whose views, followers, or revenue moved after switching.
   Even 2–3 with real numbers changes the offer.
5. **Founder's own brand content** — the reels and ads this team already edits. Free to obtain, available today,
   and it substantiates the founder story.

Text form, not screenshots — the skills read text.

## Why this blocks things

Without proof, Script QC has nothing to attach to a claim, and every script defaults to mechanism explanation
with no evidence behind it. Against a sceptical, already-burned market, that is the weakest possible position.
Item 1 alone moves Business Fundamentals off RED.
