# SQIBNT Lead Quality Scoring Guide

Score every lead on 6 dimensions (1-5 each) based on closer notes, call summaries, and observable data. The composite Lead Quality Score (LQS) is the average of all 6.

---

## Scoring Rubric

### S — Sellable
*Was the lead reachable, receptive, and engageable on the call?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | Fully engaged, open, great rapport | "Genuine conversation", "very transparent call", "really liked the team" |
| 4 | Engaged but needed warming up | Showed up, participated, some initial hesitation that resolved |
| 3 | Present but guarded | Attended but closed off, short answers, hard to open up |
| 2 | Barely engaged | Late to call, distracted, left early, refused to engage properly |
| 1 | Unreachable or hostile | No-show, ghosted, refused to join, hung up, spam/junk lead |

### Q — Qualified
*Does the lead match the ICP? Right industry, role, situation, background?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | Perfect ICP match | Business owner in right niche, right revenue range, right stage |
| 4 | Strong match with minor gap | Close to ICP but slightly different niche or stage |
| 3 | Partial match | Some ICP traits but missing key qualifiers |
| 2 | Poor match | Wrong industry, wrong stage, wrong business model |
| 1 | Completely unqualified | Not a business owner, student, employed seeking side income with no resources |

### I — Intent
*Did the lead show genuine interest in solving their problem? Research-first or impulse?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | Researched independently, arrived bought in | "Researched us independently", watched VSL, read emails, came with specific questions |
| 4 | Clear interest, consumed pre-call content | Watched VSL, engaged in GC, asked good questions on call |
| 3 | Interested but exploring | Comparing multiple options, booked calls with competitors |
| 2 | Low intent, impulse booking | Saw ad that morning, hadn't watched VSL, "just finding out what you do" |
| 1 | No real intent | Shopping around with no plan to buy, price-checking, spam |

### B — Budget
*Can they afford the offer? Is financial capacity present?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | PIF (paid in full), no financial objection | "$5,800 PIF", "not attached to the money" |
| 4 | Can afford it, chose payment plan by preference | Budget available, opted for PP for cash flow management |
| 3 | Tight but workable | Needs PP, some hesitation on price, but has access to funds |
| 2 | Financially stretched | Limited funds, under minimum threshold, needs to wait for payday/tax return |
| 1 | Cannot afford | Under $1k available, in significant debt, financial DQ |

### N — Need
*Do they have a real, urgent problem the offer solves?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | Acute pain, clear problem-solution fit | "Goal: financial freedom", "wants to leave healthcare job", specific revenue target |
| 4 | Real need, well-articulated | Knows what they want, can describe the gap, motivated |
| 3 | Vague need, general interest | "Wants to make money online", interested but can't articulate why specifically |
| 2 | Weak need | Exploring as a hobby, no urgency, "maybe someday" |
| 1 | No need | Already has what the offer provides, or problem doesn't match offer |

### T — Timing
*Are they ready to act now?*

| Score | Description | Signals |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 5 | Ready to start immediately | "Close today", "when can I begin?" |
| 4 | Ready within days | Needs to check one thing, talk to partner, get paid Friday |
| 3 | Ready within weeks | Wants to start next month, waiting on a specific event |
| 2 | Months away | "Start in summer", "after I get a job", "when things settle down" |
| 1 | No timeline | No plan to act, "just looking", indefinite |

## Scoring from Limited Data

Not every lead will have detailed closer notes. Score with what you have:

| Data available | How to score |
|---------------|-------------|
| Full closer notes + call summary | Score all 6 dimensions with evidence |
| Closer notes only (brief) | Score S, I, T from notes. Estimate B, Q, N from outcome and context. Flag estimates. |
| Outcome only (no notes) | Score S and T from outcome (no-show = S:1, T:1). Mark other dimensions as "insufficient data". Calculate partial LQS from available scores. |
| Cortana/Hyros data only (no notes) | Can only score from outcome status and revenue data. LQS will be partial. Flag that qualitative scoring requires closer notes. |

**When in doubt, score conservatively.** A slightly low score on a good lead is less harmful than a high score on a bad lead — the former loses nothing, the latter could cause budget to be allocated toward a bad-lead-producing creative.
