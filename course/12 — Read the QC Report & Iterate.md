# Step 12 — Read The QC Report & Iterate

**Time:** 10 min
**Goal:** Understand the 13-check QC system so you know when to push back, when to accept, and when to kill a concept.

---

## The 13 QC Checks

Every script runs through these. Each is rated **PASS**, **NEEDS WORK**, or **FAIL**.

### Per-Script Checks

| # | Check | What It's Looking For |
|---|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | Specificity | Could this script be for anyone, or is it clearly for ONE persona? |
| 2 | Hook Quality | Scroll-stopping, audience-qualifying, awareness-level appropriate, distinct from other hooks |
| 3 | Avatar Alignment | Does the script match the assigned persona's pains, desires, triggers, language? |
| 4 | AI-isms Detection | Scans for 13 specific AI language patterns |
| 5 | Body Structure | Does the body structure match the awareness level (Unaware = educational first, Product-Aware = proof-stack)? |
| 6 | Open Loop | Is there a sustained curiosity gap from hook to CTA? |
| 7 | Value Equation | Are all 4 levers covered (dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, effort)? |
| 8 | Traffic Qualification | Does each hook contain a qualifier that filters out non-ICP? |
| 9 | CTA Quality | [Action] + [Vehicle] + [Specific deliverable]? |
| 10 | Recordability | Word count in range, sentences under 15 words, speakable in one take? |

### Batch-Level Checks

| # | Check | What It's Looking For |
|---|-------|----------------------|
| 11 | Format Diversity | Are multiple formats represented or all the same? |
| 12 | Angle Diversity | Are scripts attacking different angles, or saying the same thing different ways? |
| 13 | Awareness Distribution | Is cold traffic (Unaware + Problem-Aware) at least 40% of the batch? |

---

## How To Use The Report

### If Everything Passes

Ship it. Record, edit, launch. Move to bottleneck analysis (Step 12).

### If You See "NEEDS WORK"

Read Claude's specific feedback. Usually easy to fix. Tell Claude:

```
Fix Script 2. The feedback says Hook B lacks a qualifier. Rewrite Hook B
so it contains a specific term that filters out non-ICP viewers. Keep
the same entry point and emotional angle — just add the qualifier.
```

Re-run QC after the fix.

### If You See "FAIL"

One of two options:

**Option 1 — Fix It**
If Claude flagged something specific (e.g., "Hook C uses AI-ism pattern 7: The Bonus Reveal"), the fix is clear. Ask for a rewrite.

**Option 2 — Kill It**
If multiple checks are failing and the script feels off, scrap it and ask Claude for a new storyboard. Better to kill a bad concept than revise it into mediocrity.

## The AI-isms List (Check 4)

This is the most common failure. Here are the 13 patterns:

1. **The Reframe Flip** — "It's not X, it's Y"
2. **The Rhetorical Reveal** — "The reason? It's X."
3. **The Pseudo-Intimate Opener** — "Here's the thing:"
4. **The Forced Reflection** — "Sound familiar?"
5. **The Dramatic Fragment List** — "No gimmicks. No fluff. Just results."
6. **The Upgrade Reframe** — "This isn't a course — it's a transformation."
7. **The Bonus Reveal** — "And the best part?"
8. **The Welcome Mat** — "Say goodbye to X and hello to Y."
9. **The Inclusive Qualifier** — "Whether you're a [X] or a [Y]..."
10. **The Self-Answering Question Chain** — "What if you could X? What if Y?"
11. **The Empathy Mirror** — "You've tried everything. You're exhausted. You just want..."
12. **The CTA Cushion** — "Ready to [outcome]?"
13. **The Authority Pivot** — "In fact, ..." "You see, ..."

If your script has any of these, it sounds like AI wrote it. Fix it.

---

## The Friend Test

The hardest test, and the most important. Read every line of the script out loud and ask:

**"Would this specific persona say this to a friend?"**

If the answer is no for any sentence, rewrite it.

The fastest way to non-AI copy: say the idea out loud first. Record it. Transcribe what you said. That's your raw material. Tighten it slightly. Ship it.

---

## When To Override The QC

Rare, but valid. Sometimes the QC flags something that's actually a deliberate choice.

**Example:** The QC flags "This isn't a course — it's a transformation" as Pattern 6 (Upgrade Reframe). But in your script, it's being spoken by a real person in a testimonial. That's fine — keep it.

**Rule:** Override only when you can articulate exactly why the flag doesn't apply. If you can't defend it, don't override it.

---

## Re-Running QC

After revisions, ask:

```
Re-run QC on the revised script. Tell me which checks now pass and
if anything new has broken.
```

Iterate until everything passes. Then ship.
