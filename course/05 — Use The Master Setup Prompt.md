# Step 05 — Use The Master Setup Prompt

**Time:** 2–3 hours (pause and resume any time)
**Goal:** Let Claude run the entire setup for you.

---

## What You're About To Do

Everything from here on is handled inside Claude. No more context-switching between docs and the chat. Claude:

- Verifies your workspace
- Interviews you to fill in your business + ad account context
- Walks you through uploading sales calls, winning ads, proof, and competitor research
- Runs the full Creative Strategy (personas, scoring, micro personas, strategy map)
- Writes 5 QC'd ad scripts from the brief
- Calculates your KPIs and locks in your benchmarks
- Explains your ongoing operations rhythm
- Optionally builds a custom skill for you

You follow along and answer questions. That's it.

---

## How To Use It

1. Open **`MASTER SETUP PROMPT.md`** in the course folder
2. Copy the entire prompt inside the code block
3. Paste it into Claude
4. Answer the questions one at a time
5. Pause whenever you want — Claude saves progress automatically

---

## Rules For Good Answers

The quality of your inputs = the quality of everything Claude produces. Follow these rules:

**Be specific.**
Bad: "I help businesses grow."
Good: "$2,500/month retainer, 40-80 qualified leads per month for construction trades — roofers, solar, bathroom remodels."

**Don't make things up.**
If you don't know your ROAS, say "I don't know." Claude will flag it as a gap and fix it in the KPI phase. Don't guess.

**Push back when Claude gets it wrong.**
If a persona feels off, tell Claude. If a hook sounds too polished, tell Claude. The system is only as good as the feedback loop.

---

## Pausing & Resuming

Say **"pause"** any time. Claude saves your progress to `memory/open-loops.md`.

When you come back:
1. Open Claude Code in your workspace folder
2. Type: `resume guided setup`
3. Claude picks up where you left off

---

## The Hard Gates

Claude will stop the setup (not skip, not fake it) if any of these aren't met:

- **Less than 5 sales call transcripts** — Non-negotiable. Go get more.
- **No offer details in your context** — Fill these in before proceeding.
- **No ad account benchmarks** (even rough ones) — Ballpark is fine, but you need something.

If Claude stops you, go fix the blocker and come back. This is the system protecting you from garbage outputs.
