# Step 04 — Asset Inventory (Pre-Flight Checklist)

**Time:** 30 min (the check) + however long it takes you to gather what's missing
**Goal:** Confirm you have every asset needed to run this workspace at a professional level. If you don't, gather the gaps BEFORE pasting the Master Setup Prompt.

---

## Why This Module Exists

The Master Setup Prompt is only as good as the data you feed it. If you paste the prompt without the inputs, Claude can't produce a real creative strategy, can't build real personas, and can't write ads that actually convert.

Most people skip this step. Their outputs end up generic. Don't be one of those people.

This checklist gives you the exact assets to gather, why each one matters, what "enough" looks like, and where to get them if you don't have them.

At the end, you rate yourself **RED / YELLOW / GREEN**. If you're RED or YELLOW on any critical asset, go fix it before you paste the Master Setup Prompt.

## 1. Voice of Customer Data — HARD GATE

This is the difference between generic ads and ads that convert. The system needs to see how your customers actually talk — their exact words, objections, triggers, desires — so it can produce copy that sounds like them, not like marketing.

### What You Need

- [ ] **20+ sales call transcripts** — mix of won AND lost deals, diverse customer types, within last 6 months
- [ ] **Customer survey data** (if you have it) — intake forms, NPS responses, post-purchase feedback
- [ ] **Support logs / emails** (if you have them) — common questions, complaints, praise
- [ ] **Online reviews** — yours AND competitors (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Google, Facebook)
- [ ] **Social media comments / community posts** — threads where your audience discusses their problem before they know about you

### Why Each One Matters

| Asset | What It Reveals |
|-------|-----------------|
| Sales calls | Real objections, triggers that made them book, their exact language |
| Survey data | Self-reported desires and barriers (often different from what they say on calls) |
| Support logs | Post-purchase reality — what's working, what's breaking |
| Reviews | Voice of comparison shoppers — how they evaluate vs. competitors |
| Community posts | **Unaware-level language** — how they talk before knowing your solution exists |

### What "Enough" Looks Like

- **Minimum viable:** 5 sales call transcripts
- **Operational:** 10-15 sales calls + some support logs OR reviews
- **Excellent:** 20+ sales calls + survey data + support logs + reviews + community scraping

### If You Don't Have Enough

- **No sales calls:** Start recording today. Use Fathom (free tier works). Come back when you have 5+.
- **Calls aren't recorded:** Check if your setter/closer has access to Zoom cloud recordings or Grain/Gong. If not, start now and rescope Master Setup for 4 weeks out.
- **No surveys:** Send an intake form to your last 10 closed clients. Single question: "What was going on in your life/business that made you reach out to us?" Takes 1 day.
- **No reviews or community data:** Go to competitor ad comment sections. Scrape 100+ comments. This is where Unaware-level language hides.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — Less than 5 sales calls. **Do not proceed.** Go gather.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — 5-9 sales calls, no other voice-of-customer data. Proceed but flag thin data.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — 10+ sales calls + at least 2 other voice-of-customer sources.

## 2. Business Fundamentals — HARD GATE

Without these, the system can't understand what you actually sell or who you sell to.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Offer details** — what you sell, pricing, payment structure, what the customer gets
- [ ] **Unique mechanism** — the how that makes your approach different from everyone else
- [ ] **ICP definition** — demographics, firmographics, situation, triggers
- [ ] **Proof assets** — minimum 5 testimonials, case studies, or results with specific numbers
- [ ] **Known objections** — top 3-5 reasons people don't buy on calls
- [ ] **Founder/brand story** (if relevant to marketing)

### What "Enough" Looks Like

- **Minimum:** You can answer all 6 items in 1-2 sentences each
- **Operational:** You have proof assets with specific numbers and testimonials with customer photos
- **Excellent:** You have documented avatars, case studies with full before/after data, and a written story

### If You're Missing Things

- **No proof assets:** Ask your 10 best clients for a written testimonial. Offer them an incentive. Takes 1 week.
- **Can't articulate unique mechanism:** This is a bigger problem than ads. Do [offer homework] — read Hormozi's $100M Offers, re-watch your best sales calls, identify what you ACTUALLY do differently. Don't run ads at scale until this is resolved.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — Can't answer 3+ items specifically. Fix the business before running ads.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — Can answer but no documented proof assets. Gather before proceeding.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — All 6 items documented with proof.

## 3. Ad Account Data

Only applies if you're already running ads. If you're brand new, skip to Category 4.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Ad account access** — Meta Business Manager access (or credentials for whoever manages)
- [ ] **Current performance metrics** — spend, CPM, CTR, CPR, ROAS (at least rough numbers)
- [ ] **Past 6 months of data** — able to export campaign/ad-level reports
- [ ] **Attribution window** — what you're currently tracking with
- [ ] **Current campaign structure** — how campaigns/ad sets/ads are organised
- [ ] **Current naming convention** (if any)

### Why This Matters

Without this data, the Bottleneck Analysis skill can't diagnose anything. You'll just stare at numbers with no context.

### What "Enough" Looks Like

- **Minimum:** You know your current spend and roughly what's working vs. not
- **Operational:** You can pull CSV reports from Meta with campaign/ad-level data
- **Excellent:** Proper naming conventions, clean account structure, 6+ months of trackable history

### If You're Missing Things

- **No proper account structure:** This is fixable. The Media Buying SOP (produced after setup) will document exactly how to restructure.
- **No naming convention:** Same — SOP fixes this.
- **Account disabled/banned:** Get a new account (or use another team member's) before running ads. Parallel path while building creative.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — No ad account access, can't pull any data. Get access first.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — Have access, unclear on metrics or structure. Proceed but prepare to restructure.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Clean data, clear structure, 6 months of history.

## 4. Market Intelligence

Tells the system what's already out there so you can differentiate.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Top 3-5 competitors identified** — the ones running ads in your space
- [ ] **Their active ads documented** — from Meta Ad Library
- [ ] **Their angles, formats, hooks catalogued** — what are they saying, how
- [ ] **Their offers documented** — what they charge, what they include
- [ ] **Your differentiation articulated** — one sentence per competitor on how you're different

### How To Get It

1. Go to **facebook.com/ads/library**
2. Search each competitor — document every active ad they're running
3. For each: note hook, angle, format, offer, positioning
4. Save to `knowledge-base/research/competitors/competitor-[name].md`

### What "Enough" Looks Like

- **Minimum:** 3 competitors, 3-5 ads each documented
- **Operational:** 5 competitors, 10+ ads each, positioning analysis
- **Excellent:** Running tracking on competitors' ads weekly (Foreplay, Motion, Magic Brief, or manual)

### If You're Missing This

You need to build it. No shortcut. Takes 2-4 hours for a proper first pass.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — No competitor research. Build it now.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — Know your competitors but haven't documented their ads.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Full competitor map documented.

## 5. Performance History

Only applies if you've run ads before.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Winning ad scripts** — text form of your top 3-5 performers
- [ ] **Losing ad scripts** (if documented) — so we don't repeat mistakes
- [ ] **Hit rate data** — how many ads tested vs. how many won
- [ ] **Scaling history** — which ads scaled profitably and which broke at higher spend

### Why Text-Form Winners Matter

The Script Generator skill mirrors the tone, pacing, and structure of your winners. Without text versions, it has no reference for what sounds like YOU. Video links don't work — Claude can't read them yet.

### If You Don't Have These

- **Running ads but no text winners documented:** Spend 1 hour transcribing your top 3 performers into text files. Drop in `knowledge-base/winning-ads/`.
- **New account, no winners:** That's fine. Create `knowledge-base/winning-ads/NEW-ACCOUNT.md` with a note. System will lean heavier on competitor references.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — Running ads but no text winners documented AND no intent to document.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — Running ads, no text winners yet, but will spend 1 hour this week to document.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Top 3-5 winners in text form, with performance metrics noted.

## 6. Tracking Infrastructure

This determines how clearly you can see what's working.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Attribution system** — Cortana, HiROS, or manual UTM tracking
- [ ] **Lead-to-ad attribution** — for lead forms, know which ad each lead came from
- [ ] **Sales data merge** — lead data + closed deals merged so you know CAC per ad
- [ ] **Reporting sheet** — weekly metric tracking template

### Why This Matters

Meta's in-platform metrics can under- or over-attribute by 30%+. Without third-party tracking, kill/scale decisions are guesses.

### Tool Recommendations

- **Under $10K/month ad spend:** Manual UTMs + Google Sheets merge. Free.
- **$10K-50K/month:** Cortana (~$300/mo) or HiROS starter tier. Worth it.
- **$50K+/month:** HiROS full tier. Required.

### If You Don't Have This

- **Lead forms without attribution:** Easy fix — download Meta lead exports include ad ID. Merge with sales data manually. 30-min setup.
- **Direct purchase without tracking:** Set up Cortana or HiROS. Start the free trial today while you gather other assets.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — No tracking at all. Won't be able to use Bottleneck Analysis properly.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — Raw Meta reports only. Manual merge works but is fragile.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Third-party attribution (Cortana/HiROS) running clean.

## 7. Creative Production Assets

What you need to actually produce the ads the system scripts.

### What You Need

- [ ] **B-roll library** — stock footage or your own footage that matches your brand
- [ ] **Testimonial footage** — video testimonials if available, raw screen recordings work too
- [ ] **Product shots / lifestyle images** — for static ads and b-roll cutaways
- [ ] **Brand guidelines** — colors, fonts, logo usage (even informal)
- [ ] **Talent** — who's going to appear in UGC/talking-head ads? You? A hired actor?
- [ ] **Editor** — who edits the videos? You, a contractor, an agency?

### If You Don't Have These

- **No talent:** Use stock UGC from BillyGene stock or FiverrUGC. Or start recording yourself selfie-style — free.
- **No editor:** Contract on Fiverr or Upwork for $20-80 per video. Or use CapCut + their AI editor.
- **No brand guidelines:** Informal is fine for beta — use consistent colors and fonts. Formalise later.

### Your Score

- 🟡 **YELLOW** is acceptable here — can fill gaps as you go.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Production pipeline in place, can produce 5 ads/week.

## 8. Team & Capacity

How much human time is actually available to run this system.

### What You Need

- [ ] **Weekly hours committed** to running ads — realistic number, not aspirational
- [ ] **Who does scripting** — you or delegate?
- [ ] **Who does editing** — you, contractor, agency?
- [ ] **Who does uploading/campaign management** — you or media buyer?
- [ ] **Who does sales** — setter + closer in place if lead gen?

### The Math

Running this system at 10K/mo spend: ~5-10 hours/week
Running at 50K/mo spend: ~15-20 hours/week
Running at 200K/mo spend: full-time role

If you don't have the hours, the system produces outputs that don't get executed. Common failure mode.

### If You're Under-Resourced

- **No time:** Don't launch. Fix capacity first. Hire, delegate, or pause something else.
- **No setter/closer (lead gen funnel):** Paid leads without follow-up = wasted spend. Book a setter before launching.

### Your Score

- 🔴 **RED** — Less than 5 hours/week available and you're the only person.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — 5-10 hours/week, no delegation yet.
- 🟢 **GREEN** — Clear roles + hours, can commit.
