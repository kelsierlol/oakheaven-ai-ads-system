# Step 08 — Load Your Sales Calls

**Time:** 10 min (plus export time from your call recording tool)
**Goal:** Get 20 diverse sales call transcripts into `knowledge-base/sales-calls/`.

---

## Why This Is The Most Important Input

Nothing else matters as much as this. Your sales calls are the richest source of voice-of-customer data you'll ever have. They contain:

- The exact words your customers use to describe their problem
- The specific objections they raise before buying
- The triggers that made them book a call
- The desires they actually chase (not what you think they chase)

The Creative Strategy skill runs on this language. Without real sales calls, you'll end up with generic personas and ads that sound like everyone else's.

**Rule:** 20 diverse calls = a strong foundation. 10 = okay. Less than 5 = system won't run properly.

---

## How To Export

### If You Use Fathom

1. Log in to Fathom
2. Go to your call library
3. Filter for calls from the last 6 months
4. Open each call → click the transcript button → copy/download as text
5. Save each as a `.txt` file in `knowledge-base/sales-calls/`

### If You Use Otter.ai

1. Export each conversation as text (`.txt` or `.docx`)
2. Save in `knowledge-base/sales-calls/`

### If You Use Zoom Recordings

1. Use a transcription service (Otter, Rev, or paste audio into Claude)
2. Export as text and save in the folder

### If You Don't Record Calls

Start recording. Today. Use Fathom (free tier works). Come back in a month when you have 20.

---

## How To Pick The Right 20

Don't just grab your 20 best calls. You want diversity:

- **Mix of wins and losses.** Losses contain the best objection data.
- **Different customer types.** If you sell to both small and big clients, include both.
- **Different funnels.** If you have Facebook leads AND referrals, include some of each.
- **Different times.** Don't use 20 calls all from the same week — bias.
- **Recent (last 6 months).** Older data is less representative.

---

## File Naming

Name each file so you can reference it later. Suggested format:

```
2026-03-15 — [Customer Name] — [Won/Lost] — [Source].txt
```

Example:
```
2026-03-15 — Mike (Bathroom Co) — Won — Facebook.txt
2026-03-22 — Sarah (Roofing Co) — Lost — Referral.txt
```

This makes it easy to spot patterns later.

```
strategy. Flag any concerns.
```

Claude will tell you if you have enough variety or if you're heavy on one customer type.
