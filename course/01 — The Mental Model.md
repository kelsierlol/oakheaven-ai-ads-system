# Step 01 — The Mental Model

**Time:** 5 min
**Goal:** Understand what you're about to build before you build it.

---

## The Three Pillars Of Ads

Every ad account problem falls into one of three buckets. If you can't identify which bucket a problem is in, you can't fix it.

1. **Concept Creation** — What do we put in front of people? This includes research, personas, angles, scripts, and formats. The "what" of the ad.

2. **Bottleneck Analysis** — When the ad is running, what's working and what's not? Where exactly is the funnel breaking? What do we kill, scale, or fix?

3. **Data Integrity** — Are we looking at the right numbers? Is our attribution accurate? Do we actually know our ROAS per ad, per angle, per persona?

Most people running ads skip #1 and #3 and just stare at #2, then can't figure out why nothing changes. You won't be one of those people.

## What This System Replaces

- The market research phase of launching a new campaign
- The scripting process (hook ideation, body structure, CTA writing)
- The quality check before scripts go to production
- Weekly performance diagnosis
- Financial modelling for unit economics

---

## What This System Does NOT Replace

- **Your judgment.** The AI suggests. You approve. You override when you know better.
- **Your data.** Garbage in, garbage out. The system is only as good as what you feed it.
- **Execution.** Claude writes scripts — you still need to record, edit, upload, and launch.
- **Sales.** This gets you qualified leads and good creative. You still need to close.

---

## The Core Loop

```
Data → Creative Strategy → Scripts → QC → Launch → Bottleneck Analysis
  ↑                                                        │
  └────────────── Feedback Loop ──────────────────────────┘
```

Run this loop continuously. Every bottleneck analysis reveals new gaps. Those gaps become input for the next creative strategy run. The system gets smarter with every cycle.
