# Onboarding — Client Inputs Checklist

Everything we need from you to build your AI Ads System. The more you give us, the sharper the system performs. Items marked **REQUIRED** are hard gates — we can't build without them.

---

## 1. Your Business & Offer (REQUIRED)

These populate your `context/business.md` — the system's understanding of who you are and what you sell.

- [ ] **Offer overview** — What you sell, who it's for, what the customer gets, pricing structure
- [ ] **Unique mechanism** — What makes your approach different from competitors (the "how")
- [ ] **Proof assets** — Testimonials, case studies, results, screenshots, before/afters (minimum 3)
- [ ] **Known objections** — The top 3-5 reasons people DON'T buy on sales calls
- [ ] **Founder/brand story** — Why this exists, your credibility, personal journey (if used in marketing)
- [ ] **Brand voice notes** — How you sound in marketing. Casual? Professional? Funny? Give examples of writing you like

---

## 2. Sales Call Recordings or Transcripts (REQUIRED — minimum 5)

These are the most important input. They're where we find the real customer language, objections, desires, and triggers that make ads convert. More = better. Ideal is 10+.

- [ ] **Minimum 5 sales call recordings or transcripts**
  - Must be recent (within last 3-6 months)
  - Must be diverse (different customer types, not 5 calls with the same persona)
  - Format: Audio files (we'll transcribe) OR text transcripts
  - Include: won deals AND lost deals (lost deals often have the best objection data)

---

## 3. Customer Data (REQUIRED)

This tells us who is ACTUALLY buying — not who you think is buying.

- [ ] **Customer demographics** — Age, gender, location, income level, profession breakdown
  - Source: CRM export, payment processor data, or application/intake form data
- [ ] **Application or intake form responses** — If you have an application form, export all responses
- [ ] **Customer survey data** (if you have it) — NPS surveys, post-purchase surveys, feedback forms

---

## 4. Ad Account Access & History (REQUIRED if running ads)

This populates your `context/ad-account.md` and gives the system performance context.

- [ ] **Ad account access** — Viewer access to your Meta (Facebook) ad account
- [ ] **Current monthly ad spend** — How much you're spending per month
- [ ] **Performance benchmarks** — What's your target CPR, ROAS, CPC? (If you don't know, we'll set them)
- [ ] **Winning ad scripts** — Text versions of your best-performing ads (we need the copy, not just the video)
  - Minimum: your top 3-5 performers
  - Include: what made them work (if you know), how long they ran, key metrics
- [ ] **Failed ad scripts** (if you have them) — What you've tested that didn't work. Saves us from repeating mistakes

*If you've never run ads before, skip this section. We'll start from scratch.*

---

## 5. Competitor Research (REQUIRED)

This feeds into creative strategy — finding the gaps competitors aren't filling.

- [ ] **Top 3-5 competitors** — Who else is advertising to your audience?
- [ ] **Competitor ad examples** — Screenshots or links to their ads (use Meta Ad Library: facebook.com/ads/library)
  - What angles are they using?
  - What formats (video, static, carousel)?
  - What hooks are they leading with?
- [ ] **What makes you different from each competitor** — In one sentence per competitor

---

## 6. Unit Economics (REQUIRED for media buying skills)

These feed the KPI Tracker and Media Buying SOP Generator. Without these, kill/scale thresholds are guesses.

- [ ] **Revenue per customer** — Average order value, contract value, or total LTV per customer
- [ ] **Fulfilment cost per customer** — COGS, team time, software, commissions to deliver to one customer
- [ ] **Monthly overhead** — Fixed costs that don't change with customer count (rent, salaries, tools)
- [ ] **Funnel type** — Call funnel / Webinar / Low-ticket / Subscription / Direct purchase
- [ ] **Funnel conversion rates** (whichever apply):
  - Call funnels: cost per booked call, show rate, qualification rate, close rate, cash collected rate
  - Webinar funnels: cost per registration, show rate, close rate
  - Low-ticket: cost per LPV, LPV → purchase rate, OTO take rate, OTO price
  - Subscription: monthly price, average retention (months), monthly churn
- [ ] **Target ROAS** — What return on ad spend does the business need to be profitable?
- [ ] **Creative production capacity** — How many new ad concepts can you realistically produce per week?

---

## 7. Attribution & CRM Data (REQUIRED for Lead Quality Report)

Meta's in-platform numbers are unreliable. The Lead Quality Report maps ad spend to real sales outcomes — this needs off-platform truth.

- [ ] **Attribution tool** — Which platform? (Hyros / Cortana / Northbeam / TripleWhale / manual UTMs)
- [ ] **Sample export** — A recent CSV export from your attribution tool (sales + calls)
- [ ] **CRM / closer log** — Where call outcomes and closer notes live (GHL, Airtable, Sheets, etc.)
- [ ] **Call recording summaries** — AI-generated (Fathom, Read AI) or manual notes per call
- [ ] **Offer tier breakdown** — If you have multiple offers (e.g. self-serve, DFY, consulting), list each with price

*Skip this section if you're not running lead-gen / call-booking funnels.*

---

## 8. Existing Content & Assets (NICE TO HAVE)

These strengthen the system but aren't required to start.

- [ ] **Customer support logs / emails** — Common questions, complaints, praise
- [ ] **Online reviews** — Google, Trustpilot, G2, app store reviews (yours AND competitors')
- [ ] **Social media comments** — Recurring themes in comments on your posts or ads
- [ ] **Community posts** — If you have a Facebook group, Skool, Circle, etc. — top posts/questions
- [ ] **Existing content library** — Blog posts, podcasts, YouTube videos, lead magnets that explain your offer
- [ ] **Landing pages** — Current landing pages / VSLs you're sending ad traffic to
- [ ] **Previous creative briefs** — If anyone has built ad strategy docs for you before

**Option B: Loom + files**
Record a 15-20 minute Loom walking through your business, offer, and what you've tried with ads. Upload files alongside it.

**Option C: On the onboarding call**
We'll walk through everything together and pull what we need live. You'll need to have the files ready to share during the call.

## What Happens After You Send This

2. We build your custom AI Ads System workspace
3. We load your data into the knowledge base
4. We run your first Creative Strategy to generate your persona profiles
5. We script your first 20 ads with 3x hook variations each
6. Handover call: we walk you through the system and how to use it
7. You're live — group calls start, ongoing support begins

## The One Thing That Makes The Biggest Difference

**Sales call transcripts.** Everything else helps. These are essential. The difference between a system that produces generic ads and one that produces ads with real customer language that converts is the quality of the voice-of-customer data. Sales calls are the richest source of that data. Send us as many as you can.
